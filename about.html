.raadioo

Info

¶ raadioo is a project that emulates the user experience of a traditional radio receiver. Just tune a station and listen. No info about the show, no distractions. If you don't like what you hear, just tune another station.

¶ The website is designed inspired in permacomputing principles. The size is aproximately 8Kb, 0.0002% of the size of the NTS website (40Mb).

¶ Made with love by sonomayrit.

¶ Missing some stations? Make your own raadioo! :)
